Catppuccin Mocha is a dark palette: the terminal sits on `#1e1e2e` and writes in `#cdd6f4`, a contrast ratio of 11.3:1. All 16 ANSI entries are different colours. A selection is drawn on `#f5e0dc`.

The extension holds one file of colour settings and nothing else. It installs no
program, starts no process and asks for no approval. Install it, pick it in
Settings, and that is all it does.

## Terminal colours

| Role | Key | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `background` | `#1e1e2e` |
| Foreground | `foreground` | `#cdd6f4` |
| Cursor | `cursor-color` | `#f5e0dc` |
| Cursor text | `cursor-text` | `#1e1e2e` |
| Selection background | `selection-background` | `#f5e0dc` |
| Selection foreground | `selection-foreground` | `#1e1e2e` |

## Installing

Open the Extensions pane in the sidebar, or Settings, then Extensions, and press
**Install** next to Catppuccin Mocha. The theme is then listed in Settings, then Appearance,
under **Extension Themes**. Press its card to apply it to every window at once.

## What the extension adds

| Area | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Theme | Catppuccin Mocha, dark |
| Terminal | 16 ANSI colours, background and foreground |
| Programs | none |
